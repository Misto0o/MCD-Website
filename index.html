📜Rules

  • Only items that can be obtained through normal gameplay and are theoretically possible in-game may be used. Extremely rare combinations are allowed, but impossible items (such as a Mystery Armor with 3 perk combinations and that cannot naturally generate) are not.
  • Accounts for the power level of gear used.
  • Players must show the gear used and level played for atleast 1-3 seconds and must be CLEAR on the damage and health output of the gear and be shown (can be at the very start of the video).
  • Modded items are allowed if they don't give any insane combat advantage (Like doing the integer limit in one hit without any buildup) (arrow farming/cosmetic/gilded/visual, etc are allowed).
  • No Hitless Gameplay allowed.
  • You may switch items around as long as rules are followed. IF you do, make sure to clearly show the changes in your video.
  • Levels not allowed: The Stronghold, Squid Coast, ???, Creeper Woods.
  • Any modifiers are allowed, but must be shown and clearly stated in the video.
  • No TAS (Tool-Assisted Speedrun) allowed.
  • Any / all builds used must tank at least 1 hit and kill at least 1 mob without the void, love medallion, or wild rage, and must finish the level with the same build whether or not you kill more mobs.
  • Must kill most mobs in the level, do not just rush to the end and kill the boss/complete the level. (If you do rush to the end, make sure to clearly show that you killed most mobs in the level and that you didn't just rush to the end and kill the boss with a super overpowered/Hitless build.)
  • You cannot bypass rules with unreasonable build swapping/the main build must stay within the rules.
